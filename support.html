Frequently asked questions

Does Richer sync across my devices?
Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud on your device. Sync happens through your own private iCloud database — there's no account to create and no server operated by the developer. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why isn't my data syncing?
Check that iCloud Drive is enabled for Richer under Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Saved to iCloud, and that both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account. Sync can also take a few minutes depending on your connection.

How does multi-currency conversion work?
Each entry stores its own original currency and amount. Richer converts everything to your chosen base currency (Settings → base currency) using exchange rates it fetches automatically from a free public rate provider. Rates refresh automatically about once a day.

How is my equity/RSU value calculated?
Vested units × your own estimated price per unit. This is intentionally a simple estimate — Richer doesn't model vesting schedules, strike prices, or tax treatment.

How do I delete an entry, or all my data?
Tap into any entry and use the delete button at the bottom of its detail page. To remove everything, delete the app — this clears local data; if iCloud sync was on, you can also remove Richer's data from iCloud via Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your device.

Can I get a weekly reminder to update my numbers?
Yes — turn on the weekly reminder in Settings and pick a day and time. It's a local notification; nothing is sent anywhere to make it work.

常见问题

Richer 能在多台设备间同步吗？
可以，前提是设备登录了 iCloud。同步完全通过你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库完成——不需要注册账号，也没有开发者运营的服务器。详见隐私政策。

为什么我的数据没有同步？
请检查「设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud → 保存到 iCloud 云盘」里 Richer 是否开启，以及两台设备是否登录了同一个 iCloud 账号。同步也可能因网络情况需要几分钟才能生效。

多币种换算是怎么算的？
每个条目都存了自己的原始币种和金额。Richer 会用自动获取的汇率，把所有条目换算成你在「设置」里选择的主币种，汇率大约每天自动刷新一次。

期权/RSU 的估值是怎么算的？
已归属份数 × 你自己填写的自估单价。这是刻意做的粗粒度估算——Richer 不处理归属排期、行权价或税务相关计算。

怎么删除一条记录，或者删除所有数据？
点进任意一条记录，在详情页底部有删除按钮。如果想清空所有数据，删除这个 App 即可——这会清掉本地数据；如果之前开过 iCloud 同步，还可以在设备的「设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud → 管理账户存储空间」里移除 Richer 在 iCloud 里的数据。

可以设置每周提醒更新数据吗？
可以——在「设置」里开启每周提醒，选好星期几和时间即可。这是纯本地通知，不会向任何地方发送数据。
