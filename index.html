Richer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Richer is a personal net worth tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, receive, or have access to any of your data. Richer has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no backend server operated by us.

Data stored on your device and iCloud

Everything you enter in Richer — cash balances, investments, equity/RSU grants, liabilities, notes, and your settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you are signed in to iCloud, this data also syncs through your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit) so it's available across your own devices. This sync is end-to-end within Apple's iCloud system — the developer never sees this data, and it is not stored on any server we control.

Currency exchange rates

To convert between currencies, Richer fetches exchange rates from the free, public Frankfurter API. Only currency codes are sent in this request — no personal or financial data leaves your device as part of this request.

Notifications

If you enable the weekly reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device using Apple's notification framework. No data is sent anywhere to make this work.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. If iCloud sync was enabled, you can also remove the app's data from iCloud via Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your device.

Children's privacy

Richer does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date above will change. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to wuyanbo.joey@gmail.com, or see the Support page.

Richer — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 27 日

Richer 是一款个人净资产追踪 App。以下说明 App 如何处理你的数据。

我们收集的数据

开发者不收集、不接收、也无法访问你的任何数据。Richer 不包含任何数据分析或广告 SDK，也没有由我们运营的后端服务器。

存储在你设备和 iCloud 上的数据

你在 Richer 中录入的所有内容——现金余额、投资、期权/RSU、负债、备注以及各项设置——都通过 Apple 的 SwiftData 框架保存在你的设备本地。如果你登录了 iCloud，这些数据还会通过你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库（Apple CloudKit）同步，以便在你自己的多台设备间使用。这个同步过程完全在 Apple 的 iCloud 体系内完成——开发者永远看不到这些数据，数据也不会存储在我们控制的任何服务器上。

汇率数据

为了实现多币种换算，Richer 会从免费、公开的 Frankfurter API 获取汇率。该请求只会发送币种代码，不会有任何个人或财务数据随请求离开你的设备。

通知

如果你开启了每周提醒，提醒会通过 Apple 的通知框架在你设备本地调度，整个过程不涉及任何数据的发送。

数据删除

删除 App 会移除本地存储的所有数据。如果开启过 iCloud 同步，你也可以在设备的「设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud → 管理账户存储空间」中移除 App 在 iCloud 中的数据。

儿童隐私

由于 Richer 不收集任何数据，因此也不会有意收集包括儿童在内的任何用户数据。

政策变更

如本政策发生变化，上方的更新日期会随之更新。变更后继续使用本 App 即表示你接受更新后的政策。

联系方式

关于本政策的问题，可发邮件到 wuyanbo.joey@gmail.com，或查看支持页面。
