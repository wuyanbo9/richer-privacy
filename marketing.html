Richer

A simple way to track your net worth — cash, investments, equity & RSU, and liabilities, all in one place.

Multi-currency Synced via your own iCloud No account, no ads

Not a bookkeeping app

No need to log every transaction — just check in once a week.

💰

Four categories, one total

Cash, investments, equity/RSU grants, and liabilities — each tracked and subtotaled separately, rolled up into one net worth number.

🌍

Multi-currency, automatic

Enter each account in its own currency. Richer fetches live exchange rates and converts everything to your base currency automatically.

📈

See the trend, not just the number

A clean trend chart on the overview, plus a mini history and delta for every single entry — so you always know what changed since last time.

🔔

Weekly reminders

Turn on a gentle weekly nudge to update your balances, so your net worth stays current without becoming a chore.

☁️

Synced through your own iCloud

No account to create. Your data syncs across your devices through your own private iCloud database — the developer never has access to it.

🔒

No ads, no tracking

No analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, nothing collected. Richer's only network request is a free public exchange-rate lookup.
